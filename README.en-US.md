# MomAI Vision

The MomAI Vision extension gives eyes to your assistant. It allows MomAI to connect to your webcam or security cameras to see the environment, describe what is happening, and alert you to important events.

## Smart eyes to protect and assist your daily routine

Whether to check who arrived at the gate, see what's happening in the yard, or simply ask what the camera sees, MomAI Vision delivers an intuitive and secure visual experience.

### Main Features

- **Scene Description**: Ask the assistant to look at the camera and describe in natural language what is in front of it.
- **Motion & Person Detection**: Receive desktop alerts whenever someone or an object is detected in the monitored area.
- **Custom Visual Questions**: Configure automatic questions, such as alerting if the gate is open or if someone is at the back door.
- **Live Panel & Snapshot Gallery**: Watch your cameras in real time and review captured snapshots directly on the lateral bar.
- **100% Local and Private**: All computer vision runs directly on your machine. No images or video feeds are sent to external servers.

## What you can ask MomAI

- "MomAI, what are you seeing right now?"
- "Take a snapshot from the garage camera."
- "Alert me when someone arrives at the front door."
- "Notify me if there is motion in the backyard."
- "Pause garage camera monitoring."

## How to Use

1. Open MomAI and click on the Cameras icon in the sidebar.
2. Select your local webcam or enter your security camera network address (RTSP/ONVIF).
3. Enable the cameras you want to monitor.
4. You can now ask MomAI to take snapshots, describe scenes, or monitor areas in real time.

## Privacy & Security

Your home privacy is the highest priority. All image processing happens locally on your physical device. Videos and photos are never uploaded to the cloud and remain under your exclusive control.
